# Reports/ — index and provenance

Reference documents. **Read-only**: nothing there is generated by this repo, and
docs/assessment_conventions.md rule 8 keeps their contents unedited (the directory is write-protected, so
this index lives in `docs/` rather than beside them).

Filenames were normalised to `YYYY-MM_what.pdf` on 2026-08-29 and completed on 2026-08-31, dated by the document's
own date rather than the download, so the directory sorts chronologically. The delivery
name each arrived under is recorded here so provenance is not lost.

## Not present, and needed

  guidelines points to for the four risk categories and their scoring.
- The **draft template tables for the SSC tables** that the crab checklist links,

  authors. Executive Summary Tables 1–3 cannot be built to format without them.

  minute leads to check these as well as the CPT and SSC reports, because the
  Council sometimes endorses items outside the specifications.
- The **2026 ESP** (Fedewa and Shotwell 2026), cited as Appendix A and as the source
  of the risk table's ecosystem and fishery-informed rows. Only the 2025 ESP is here.

## Extraction note

`Reports/` PDFs come in two encodings and one extractor does not handle both. The
groundfish guidelines defeat the CID/ToUnicode path in
`scratchpad/pdfany.py` — it returns image data — but `pypdf.PdfReader(...).pages[i]
.extract_text()` reads them correctly. Check the first 200 characters of any
extraction before trusting a "not found" result.
